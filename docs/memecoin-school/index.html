What’s inside

A real terminal

Live Solana token discovery, candle charts, liquidity, holders, token authorities and a research browser in resizable panels you arrange yourself. Every number says where it came from and how old it is.

Paper trading that doesn’t flatter you

Your paper wallet starts with what you plan to deposit after the program. Orders fill at the price when they would have landed, pay impact, pool and network fees, and sometimes fail, just as real swaps do.

Research that marks unknowns as unknown

Freeze and mint authorities, restrictive token extensions, sell routes, pulled liquidity and concentrated supply, each with its evidence and limits. A serious warning is never outweighed by a good chart.

A journal that keeps you honest

Every trade and every skipped trade, with your plan, costs and mistakes. Win rate, expectancy, drawdown and fee burden, with a warning whenever the sample is too small to mean anything.

The seven-day program

A short questionnaire sets your starting level, your practice balance and which topics come first. Each day unlocks when you’ve mastered the last one, not when a timer runs out.

  1. Fundamentals. The terminal, SOL and tokens, market cap versus liquidity, every cost in a swap. Free.
  2. Research. Verifying the mint, authorities, liquidity, holders, deployers and volume quality.
  3. Scam detection. Rug pulls, honeypots, paid promotion, and logging the trades you walk away from.
  4. Entries and exits. Momentum, breakouts and pullbacks defined precisely, and a written plan before every trade.
  5. Risk management. Sizing from a risk budget, losing streaks, costs and limits the app enforces.
  6. Strategy validation. Objective rules, backtests with costs, overfitting and out-of-sample checks.
  7. Independent assessment. Research, size, decide, skip and review on your own.

After the program, the academy continues with 40+ lessons in nine modules: Solana mechanics, bonding curves and AMMs, on-chain and social research, fraud, psychology, market history, what large studies say about active traders, and record-keeping for taxes.

What we won’t tell you

That you’ll get rich, that our course turns anyone into a great trader, or that a token is safe. Most memecoins lose nearly all their value, and large studies of active traders find most of them lose money. This school is built to help you see the risks clearly, practise without losing money, and make decisions based on evidence, including the decision not to trade.
