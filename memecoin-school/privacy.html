Privacy

Last updated: 21 October 2026. This explains what S.T.U.M.F’s MemeCoin Trading School (“the school”, “we”) collects, why, and what you control.

What we never collect

  • Wallet recovery phrases or private keys. External wallets (Phantom, Solflare) sign in their own apps. The optional built-in wallet in the desktop app creates and keeps its keys on your computer, encrypted with your password; they are never sent to us, to analytics or to any AI service.
  • Card numbers. Card payments are handled entirely by Stripe.
  • Tracking or advertising data. The school has no ad trackers and no third-party analytics.

What stays on your device

Without an account, everything (your questionnaire answers, course progress, paper wallet, journal, research notes, layouts and settings) is stored only in this browser or app. Erase it any time in Account → Your data.

What we store when you sign in (accounts are optional and not yet switched on)

  • Your email address, to sign you in and send receipts and the purchase thank-you email.
  • Your questionnaire answers, course progress, paper wallet, journal, research notes and saved layouts, so they sync across your devices. Each record is tied to your account and protected by row-level security: no other customer can read or change it.
  • Purchase records: amount, method, time and, for SOL payments, the public transaction signature. For card payments, Stripe’s checkout session ID.
  • A security log of account and payment events, without secrets.

Your wallet’s public address appears in on-chain payments you make to us, as it does for everyone on Solana. We never publish customers’ addresses, trades or results.

Services we use

Supabase (accounts and database), Stripe (card payments), Resend (transactional email), and public Solana and market-data services (Solana RPC, Jupiter, DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, PumpPortal), which your device contacts directly to show prices and build transactions. Those services see your IP address as any website would.

Your controls

  • Export all your data as a file from Account → Your data.
  • Delete your account and every synced record from the same page. Deletion is immediate and permanent.
  • Optional questionnaire answers can be skipped; $0 is always an accepted amount.

Contact

Questions or requests: reply to your Stripe receipt, or contact S.T.U.M.F’s MemeCoin Trading School through its GitHub organization.
